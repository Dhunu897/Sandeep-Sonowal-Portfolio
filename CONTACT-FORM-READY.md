## 🚀 How It Works:

1. **Visitor fills the form** on your portfolio
2. **FormSubmit receives** the submission
3. **Email is sent** to your Gmail inbox
4. **You receive** all form details (name, email, subject, message)

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT - First Time Setup:

**The first time someone submits the form (or you test it):**

1. FormSubmit will send a **confirmation email** to `sandeepsonowal897@gmail.com`
2. **Check your inbox** (and spam folder just in case)
3. **Click the confirmation link** in that email
4. After confirmation, all future submissions will come directly to your inbox!
